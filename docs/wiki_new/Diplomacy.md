# Diplomacy

*How two nations stand toward each other, and everything that follows from it.*

## What this covers

Diplomacy covers the relationships nations have with one another and the agreements and conflicts that grow out of them. It includes [[Diplomatic states|diplomatic states]], [[Hostility]], [[Cease-fire|cease-fires]] and [[Peace treaty|peace treaties]], as well as deeper forms of cooperation such as [[Alliances]], [[Unions]] and [[Federation|federations]]. These relationships matter because nations remember what others have done, and that history affects what they are willing to do together later.
