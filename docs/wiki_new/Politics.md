# Politics

*What a government can do about the people inside it who want out, and what each answer costs it.*

## What this covers

Politics covers how a government responds to pressure from the people and movements inside its borders. [[Movement demands]] give that pressure a political form, [[Release valves]] provide ways of answering it, and [[Authority]] represents a government's ability to keep governing through the consequences. The choices here are not separate from secession: they are some of the principal ways a nation either relieves internal pressure or makes it worse.
