# Sagebrush Rebellion

> **This page:** written

## What it is

Sagebrush Rebellion is a separate movement rooted in Federal-land counties: Nevada entire, the Colorado high country, the Arizona strip, the Utah desert, western New Mexico. It wants return the federal land; county supremacy, and the intended political position carrying that demand is [[Anarcho-Capitalism]].

As a **Separate** movement, this movement wants its ground to leave the state that currently governs it and become a separate nation ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 39]]).

Its adjective is **autonomist**. In the movement design, the adjective describes what kind of concession can remove the want rather than what territorial result the movement is pursuing ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 43]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 11a]]).

Its homeland is 82 counties (4 core), which merge into 82 Areas (4 core). The movement's current growth ceiling is **0.45 ruled, 0.35 running. Politics ruling 41 raised it and says in as many words that the change was not applied, because a design session does not touch the data.**; an Area leaves at 0.40. Reachable under the ruling, not in the running game. The raised ceiling is what makes the line reachable, and it has not been applied.. The general Area departure line is 0.40 movement share ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 10]]).

At setup it **spawns on a roll** and its opening condition is **growing**. Opening movement presence and size are resolved before the player chooses a nation, while movements that are born later can arise during play ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 14]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 15]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 42]]).

The intended political position is [[Anarcho-Capitalism]]. The running game still assigns this movement the older **Republican — the game still carries the old six ideologies** label, because the ten-position [[The political board|political board]] has not yet replaced the six-ideology implementation ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 2]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 41]]).

If enough of an Area belongs to this movement, that ground can leave its governing nation; once the movement creates its own nation, support on the new nation's founding ground becomes loyalty rather than pressure against that government, and the movement's [[Homelands|homeland]] widens to the founding grant ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 16]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 17]]).

As with other movements, its members contribute to the movement's political pressure rather than simply disappearing into the government's support base; if internal conflict becomes military, movement members are intended to join the movement militia instead of the government army ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 6]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 7]]).
