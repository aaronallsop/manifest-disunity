# The board

*The ground itself - counties, Areas, homelands, and who governs what.*

## What this covers

The board is the physical country the rest of the game acts on. Real counties are organised into Areas, those Areas are governed by nations, and additional geographic layers describe things such as [[Homelands]], [[Military bases|military bases]] and ground that can become a [[Stateless society|stateless society]]. This section also covers how that geography is turned into the game's opening position through [[The opening roll]].
