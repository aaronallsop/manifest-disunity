# Secession

*The scoreboard of the whole game: why a region turns against the country holding it, and what it does about it.*

## What this covers

Secession is the system that turns pressure inside a nation into changes on the map. [[Grievance]] shapes the conditions in which [[Movements|movements]] grow, movements organise people around demands for a different political future, and sufficiently strong movements can challenge the nation governing their ground. This section covers those movements, the pressure behind them, and the ways other nations can become involved.
