*How the United States came apart — the chain that runs from Texas declaring itself out in spring 2034 to the board the game opens on two years later, and what each step left behind.*

## What it is

The Shattering is the collapse of the United States that creates the opening map. It begins with Texas declaring independence in spring 2034 and unfolds through wars, breakaways, regional unions and stateless regions until the game opens on 1 March 2036.

## How it works in the game

The timing is fixed: Texas declares roughly two years before the game opens, using its approaching bicentenary as the deadline for independence ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 11]]). By the opening date, the wars and political breakups that make the starting board have already happened.

The resulting geography is not simply the fifty states with new flags. City-states use their painted metropolitan regions; New Jersey, Delaware and Rhode Island disappear as opening nations; and Boston takes southern New Hampshire ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 1]]).

New England consolidates into the **United States of New England**, while Rochester-and-Buffalo takes northern New York ground and begins with a pre-signed alliance and free-trade relationship with New England ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 9]]).

[[Nations of the Shattering|Superior]] is restricted to Michigan ground — the Upper Peninsula plus the northern portion of the Lower Peninsula — rather than the wider cultural region painted across Wisconsin and Minnesota ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 10]]).

Two major successor states receive their final names in the rulings: the **Gulf Compact** and the **Allegheny Republic** ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 29]]).

Much of the detailed thirteen-step breakup sequence is explicitly a story brainstorm rather than a closed ruling. This page therefore treats the dated declaration and ruled borders/names as fixed, while presenting the intervening narrative as the current story draft rather than immutable canon.

## The story behind it

The current story draft runs as a cascade rather than one clean civil war. Texas leaves first and then fragments into rival successor governments. California fractures after the Bay Area breaks away. Deseret and Greater Idaho emerge in the interior West. Midwestern states organise the Farmers Union to protect agriculture and transport. Houston's war with Louisiana contributes to the formation of the Gulf Compact. Arkansas collapses into stateless ground, while Navajo and Lakota states emerge farther west.

Federal intervention around Washington and West Virginia helps produce Appalachia. The Deep South and Carolinas consolidate separately; Miami and Central Florida stand apart. Philadelphia, New York City, Detroit and Chicago become city-states, while Pennsylvania/New York remnants form the Allegheny Republic and northern Michigan becomes Superior. New England eventually consolidates into the United States of New England.

That sequence is the richest existing in-world account of the break-up, but the source labels it as revisable story material. The wiki should preserve that distinction whenever another page borrows one of its incidents.
