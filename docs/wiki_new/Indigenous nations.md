## What it is

The Indigenous nations are opening states whose territory is based on Native political geography rather than on the borders of the former U.S. states. The current design specifically creates the Navajo Nation and the Lakota Nation.

## How it works in the game

The [[Nations of the Shattering|Navajo Nation]] is based on the Navajo reservation in the Four Corners, not on the much larger cultural region painted elsewhere on the map ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 25]]). Ruling 3 first establishes the Navajo state as part of the Four Corners settlement and ruling 25 narrows the geography without overturning that decision ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 3]]).

The surrounding Four Corners ground is divided among fixed claims and opening-roll uncertainty. The Navajo core is one of the fixed pieces, while leftover contested ground is distributed through the setup system ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 23]]).

The [[Nations of the Shattering|Lakota Nation]] is defined as the **Butte, Brown and Pennington Areas**, about 271,000 people in the source measurement, and therefore includes Rapid City ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 53]]). This is a deliberate compromise forced by the Area map rather than a claim that the whole population of those Areas chose Lakota government.

Neither nation is currently implemented. The source also warns that the Navajo boundary was drafted from memory and must be checked against the real reservation boundary before the map is final.

## The story behind it

The Navajo Nation emerges in the interior West during the Shattering. The Lakota state is more geographically awkward: Pine Ridge and Rosebud belong in the political story, but the merged Area geography cannot isolate them cleanly. The chosen Lakota Areas therefore carry Rapid City and its large non-Lakota population into the new country. The source keeps that discomfort visible instead of pretending the map represents unanimous consent.
