## What it is

Distributism is one of the ten intended positions on [[The political board]]. It sits at collective economy · conservative morals · libertarian power — corner of the ten. It is a political position, not a party organisation and not a [[Movements|movement]].

## How it works in the game

The redesigned political board places governments, parties and movements against three axes: economy, morals and government power ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 2]]). **Distributism** occupies the fixed position described above; an ideology is a place on that board and does not drift, while a party can move from one position to another ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 40]]).

The current register assigns **7** movement(s) to this position. Those placements determine which political language carries the movement's separate territorial or policy demand, but the movement and the ideology remain different things ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 43]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 40]]).

The running game does not yet use this ten-position board. It still carries six ideologies on two axes, so any current behaviour attached to the older implementation should not be silently attributed to this redesigned position. The individual position pages therefore record the intended placement without inventing new bonuses, penalties or AI behaviour that have not been ruled.
