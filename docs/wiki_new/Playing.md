# Playing

*The turn, the single action it buys, what winning means, and what a nation is allowed to know.*

## What this covers

Playing covers the structure of a game rather than any one system inside it. A player takes control of one nation while the other nations act for themselves; each nation receives one action on its turn, and a complete cycle advances the world by one quarter of a year. This section covers [[The turn|the turn]], what the player is allowed to do and know, and the larger question of how a nation ultimately wins.
