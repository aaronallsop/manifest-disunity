# People

*The population: who they are, where they move, and how they feel about whoever governs them.*

## What this covers

People is the population underneath every border on the map. People live in Areas, hold political positions, can organise into [[Movements|movements]], and can move as conditions change around them. Changes in population therefore change the nations built on top of it: their politics, economies, movements and ultimately the pressure on their borders.
