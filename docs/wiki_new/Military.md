# Military

*Taking ground and holding it - war as a standing state between two nations, and the cost of soldiers standing on somebody else's home.*

## What this covers

Military covers the use of force between nations and the consequences of holding ground by force. [[War]] provides the relationship in which fighting happens, [[Taking ground|taking ground]] changes who governs an Area, and [[Occupation]] and [[Resistance]] determine what it means to keep territory whose people do not accept the new government. It also covers [[Civil war|civil wars]], where conflict inside a nation can break the country itself apart.
