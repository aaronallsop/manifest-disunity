# Christian Nationalism

> [!abstract] At a glance
> **A unify movement** — several states become one nation that never existed
> **Politics:** Christian Nationalism (position) · **Ground:** Southern counties over 100,000 people
> **This page:** written

## What it is

Christian Nationalism is a unify movement rooted in Southern counties over 100,000 people. It wants religious government; moral law, and the intended political position carrying that demand is [[Christian Nationalism (position)]].

As a **Unify** movement, this movement wants several existing states to combine into a new state that did not previously exist ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 39]]).

Its adjective is **ideological**. In the movement design, the adjective describes what kind of concession can remove the want rather than what territorial result the movement is pursuing ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 43]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 11a]]).

Its homeland is 255 counties (60 core), which merge into 251 Areas (60 core). The movement's current growth ceiling is **0.45**; an Area leaves at 0.40. Reachable.. The general Area departure line is 0.40 movement share ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 10]]).

At setup it **spawns on a roll** and its opening condition is **growing**. Opening movement presence and size are resolved before the player chooses a nation, while movements that are born later can arise during play ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 14]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 15]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 42]]).

The intended political position is [[Christian Nationalism (position)]]. The running game still assigns this movement the older **Conservative Nationalist — the game still carries the old six ideologies** label, because the ten-position [[The political board|political board]] has not yet replaced the six-ideology implementation ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 2]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 41]]).

A Unify movement does not merge countries automatically. Its political demand is that its government approach another nation and propose a [[Unions|union]]; asking discharges the immediate demand even if the other nation refuses ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 15]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 18]]).

As with other movements, its members contribute to the movement's political pressure rather than simply disappearing into the government's support base; if internal conflict becomes military, movement members are intended to join the movement militia instead of the government army ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 6]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 7]]).
