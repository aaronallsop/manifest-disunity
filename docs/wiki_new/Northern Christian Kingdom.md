# Northern Christian Kingdom

## What it is

Northern Christian Kingdom is a separate movement rooted in The interior Northwest: Montana, Idaho, eastern Oregon and Washington, Wyoming. It wants religious government; independence, and the intended political position carrying that demand is [[Christian Nationalism (position)]].

As a **Separate** movement, this movement wants its ground to leave the state that currently governs it and become a separate nation ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 39]]).

Its adjective is **religious**. In the movement design, the adjective describes what kind of concession can remove the want rather than what territorial result the movement is pursuing ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 43]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 11a]]).

Its homeland is 176 counties (25 core), which merge into 176 Areas (25 core). The movement's current growth ceiling is **0.40**; an Area leaves at 0.40. Reachable.. The general Area departure line is 0.40 movement share ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 10]]).

At setup it **spawns on a roll** and its opening condition is **growing**. Opening movement presence and size are resolved before the player chooses a nation, while movements that are born later can arise during play ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 14]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 15]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 42]]).

The intended political position is [[Christian Nationalism (position)]]. The running game still assigns this movement the older **Conservative Nationalist — the game still carries the old six ideologies** label, because the ten-position [[The political board|political board]] has not yet replaced the six-ideology implementation ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 2]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 41]]).

If enough of an Area belongs to this movement, that ground can leave its governing nation; once the movement creates its own nation, support on the new nation's founding ground becomes loyalty rather than pressure against that government, and the movement's [[Homelands|homeland]] widens to the founding grant ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 16]]; [[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 17]]).

As with other movements, its members contribute to the movement's political pressure rather than simply disappearing into the government's support base; if internal conflict becomes military, movement members are intended to join the movement militia instead of the government army ([[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 6]]; [[Rulings - Politics#Politics ruling 7]]).
