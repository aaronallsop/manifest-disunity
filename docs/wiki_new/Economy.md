# Economy

*Trade, transit and what a nation can pay for. Built as an alpha; its design round is open now.*

## What this covers

Economy covers what the country's [[The board|Areas]] produce, what nations can afford, and how goods and money move between them. The current game already has production, prices, treasuries, trade and transit, but the dedicated economy design round is still open. This section therefore describes an existing alpha system rather than a finished economic design.
