# Nations of the Shattering

> [!abstract] At a glance
> **System:** [[Story]]
> **In the game today:** designed only, and the gap is measurable: the designed board is 47 nations (29 new, 18 original states surviving as themselves) against the 61 the running game opens on. No Gulf Compact, Appalachia, Deep South, Carolinas, Allegheny Republic or Superior exists in data/ or content/; the Navajo Nation and the Lakota Nation do not exist as nations at all, and five border lines are still to be drawn.
> **Decided in:** Secession

*The roster of the nations the break-up leaves standing — who each one is, how it formed, the ruling that fixes its border, and the two whose ground the map cannot yet draw accurately.*

## What it is

The Nations of the Shattering are the countries left on the map when the game begins. Some are surviving American states, while others are new states, city-states, federations and successor governments created as the United States broke apart.

## How it works in the game

### Nations are drawn from real ground

A nation's territory is made from [[The board|Areas]] on the real county map. Where the Shattering creates a city-state, its country is the metro region painted on the cultural map rather than the old legal boundary of the city itself ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 1]]).

New Jersey, Delaware and Rhode Island therefore do not survive as independent opening nations, and Boston takes southern New Hampshire ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 1]]).

Several larger countries are likewise fixed by the painted cultural map. [[Nations of the Shattering|Appalachia]] uses the whole Southern Appalachia region ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 5]]). The [[Nations of the Shattering|Deep South]] is one country rather than several southern successor states ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 4]]). The Gullah coast belongs to the Carolinas, leaving the Deep South without a seaport ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 7]]). Central Florida uses the painted Central Florida region from the Georgia line south to Miami ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 8]]).

The country originally called the Rust Belt is named the **Allegheny Republic**, and the Gulf nation is the **Gulf Compact** ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 29]]).

The Gulf Compact stops at the Tennessee line. Memphis remains with the Deep South and Cairo remains with the Farmers Union so that no single country controls the whole Mississippi corridor ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 6]]). Its Gulf core is fixed while parts of its reach through the Florida panhandle can vary ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 30]]).

### The West has specially drawn borders

Colorado becomes the **Front Range Republic** rather than surviving intact as Colorado ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 3]]).

The [[Indigenous nations|Navajo Nation]] is based on the reservation in the Four Corners rather than the larger Navajo cultural region ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 25]]). Remaining Four Corners ground is divided among fixed claims and opening-roll uncertainty ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 23]]).

The [[Indigenous nations|Lakota Nation]] consists of Butte, Brown and Pennington Areas and therefore includes Rapid City ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 53]]). The source keeps this as an explicit geographic compromise rather than treating every resident of those Areas as having chosen the arrangement.

[[Nations of the Shattering|Superior]] is Michigan ground only: the Upper Peninsula plus the northern part of the Lower Peninsula ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 10]]).

### The Northeast is deliberately consolidated

All six New England states form the **United States of New England**. Rochester-and-Buffalo takes Clinton County and the Franklin–Essex–Hamilton Area; the two countries are designed to start with an alliance and free-trade agreement ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 9]]).

Boston remains part of New England and is not a claimant to restore the old United States ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 22]]). The eastern reunification claimants are Washington D.C., Philadelphia and New York City ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 20]]).

### Some nations begin without a founding movement

The Carolinas, Deep South, Central Florida and Allegheny Republic are opening nations created by the Shattering itself and have no founding movement built into them ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 27]]).

### Some successor states are reunification claimants

Several groups contain more than one surviving government claiming to be the continuation of the country that broke apart. Those governments participate in [[Reunification contests]] rather than merely existing as unrelated neighbours ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 19]]).

### What the running game currently does differently

The designed opening board contains **47 nations**, while the current game still opens with 61. Many ruled nations and borders — including the Gulf Compact, Appalachia, Deep South, Carolinas, Allegheny Republic, Superior, Navajo Nation and Lakota Nation — have not yet been built. The current board also still treats New Jersey, Delaware and Rhode Island as ordinary nations.

Several precise Area lines still need map-authoring work, and the pre-signed New England/Rochester agreements cannot yet be represented by the current diplomacy implementation.

## The story behind it

The current story begins with Texas leaving the United States in spring 2034, roughly two years before the game opens on 1 March 2036 ([[Rulings - Secession#Secession ruling 11]]). Texas fragments into five rival governments. California later fragments, Deseret and Greater Idaho emerge in the interior West, the Midwest organises around the Farmers Union, and Houston's war with Louisiana contributes to the Gulf Compact.

Arkansas becomes the first old state in the story to collapse into [[Stateless society|stateless ground]]. Navajo and Lakota states emerge in the West. Federal movement through West Virginia contributes to Appalachia; the Deep South and Carolinas consolidate separately; Miami and Central Florida stand apart. Philadelphia, New York City, Detroit and Chicago become city-states, the Allegheny Republic forms from other northeastern ground, and Superior forms in northern Michigan. New England finally consolidates into the United States of New England.

The detailed thirteen-step sequence is explicitly a **story brainstorm open to revision**, while the numbered border/name rulings are design decisions. This page keeps that distinction visible.
