# Movement demands

> [!abstract] At a glance
> **System:** [[Politics]]
> **In the game today:** designed only, and one half is genuinely new machinery that the design does not have. Measured this run: the word 'petition' appears nowhere in js/; there is no demand, no answer and no screen; a movement's stated goals are authored flavour text and nothing in the game ever changes what a movement wants; and the grant/refuse card that exists is a transit request from another nation, not a movement demand. This is the one piece both rounds 2 and 3 flag as not existing.
> **Decided in:** Military, Politics
> **This page:** not yet written

*The free, mandatory phase at the top of every turn where a movement inside your own country states what it wants and names the ground it wants, the three answers a government may give — grant it, refuse it, or tell them to wait and pay far more later — and the broken-promise clock that turns a strung-along movement into a separatist one.*
