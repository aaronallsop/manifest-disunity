The political spectrum

Three axes, three positions on each, twenty-seven places a party can stand. Every one of them has been held by somebody real — this is the reference of who. Edit anything: the names, the descriptions, the examples. Your changes are saved as you type.

Economy

Who allocates. Collective — the community or the state owns and directs. Neo-liberal — markets and private ownership decide.

Morals

What the good life looks like. Conservative — inherited custom, faith and family are the standard. Progressive — the standard is arrived at, and revised.

Government power

How much coercion the state may use on people, independent of what it is using it for. The axis most spectrums leave out.
