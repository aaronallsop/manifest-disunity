warning written inside the handoff — and the session that failed next had it in front of it. Measure the
thing instead. That is now rule 10 and fleet decision D10.

failure is what revealed the scenario was testing ruling 11a and only bites for ideological movements.

a verb"; the data showed **no movement has a verb there at all** — verbs live in the Movement Register.
The finding was right and its reason was wrong.

ruling 16 worth more than it looked; influence already counts trade partners, which is why ruling 37's
