Where this comes from. The same link data the wiki uses, in build/wiki_edges.json. Each link carries a relation, a clause and the ruling it rests on. Obsidian's graph view draws all seven relations as the same grey line, which is why it comes out a hairball; this keeps them apart.

Read it with the right amount of trust. The clauses are drafts — they were derived from the rulings, not written by hand, and the ones marked unverified have not been checked against the source by a person. A link says two pages are related; the ruling it cites is the thing that is actually true.
